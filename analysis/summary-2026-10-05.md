## Observations
- The block interval was exactly 2.00 s in both block samples.
- No block was more than about a fifth full (maximum 19.5%).
- USDC value is concentrated: the top 5 senders moved about 92% of the total on 2026-09-30 and about 78% on 2026-10-01.
- Three of the top 5 senders appear in both of the later USDC samples (0xb2cc...DC59, 0x6104...D14b and 0x787f...35E4, shortened addresses). Their owners are not verified.
- "Total moved" overcounts real volume because chained transfers are counted at every step.

## Limitations
- Short windows taken at different times of day.
- Transaction counts include automated activity.
